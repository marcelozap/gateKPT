## Non-Negotiables

- Preserve the XIV origin: dark city, visor light, cyan signal, restrained magenta, amber only as an accent.
- Do not drift into generic SaaS, crypto, trading, gaming, or startup-marketing visuals.
- Use the public labels `Published writing`, `Writing`, and `AI layers`.
- Avoid `Field log` and `Stack map` in visible user-facing UI unless Marcelo explicitly asks for them.
- Teach from the reader's experience outward. Start with what the person does, then reveal the system underneath.
- Treat pasted articles as training references, not automatically verified public claims.
- Verify current privacy, legal, product, and policy claims from primary sources before publishing them.
- Keep source articles, transcript summaries, screenshots, and images in `docs/training/source_references/`.

## Current GateKPT Teaching Direction

The AI learning path should feel inductive and intuitive:

1. I type something.
2. The system breaks it into tokens.
3. The app adds context.
4. The model generates.
5. Tools, files, search, or workflows may be used.
6. Chips move the data.
7. Power and physical infrastructure make it possible.

The reader should not feel like they are being dropped into infrastructure first. They should feel the familiar surface and then discover what is underneath it.
