# GateKPT 6-Month Apartment Roadmap

## North Star

GateKPT is not a content machine.

GateKPT is the private creative operating system for this apartment season: capture ideas fast, organize versions, study what works, make better artistic decisions, and export only when the work is strong enough.

Core loop:

```text
create fast -> save history -> learn taste -> choose better -> export when ready
```

Success by the end of six months:

- recording is reliable enough to trust every night
- Marcelo has a repeatable guitar/vocal/loop workflow
- GateKPT remembers takes, lyrics, taste lanes, song studies, momentum, and post outcomes
- the artist world is clearer: sound, visuals, voice, languages, and emotional lane
- public clips come from real creative work, not pressure to post
- at least one small body of work exists: covers, original seeds, field sounds, visual pieces, and a first EP map

## Guiding Rules

- Make the work better first; content follows the work.
- Fit and memorability beat volume.
- Covers are discovery tests, not the whole identity.
- Historical data steers decisions, but taste still leads.
- Post-worthy clips need one hook, one feeling, one visual, one difference, and one reason to respond.
- Keep the workflow small enough to use after work.

## Month 1: Trust The Capture

Goal: GateKPT must feel reliable, simple, and worth opening.

Build:

- stabilize recording and playback paths
- keep the recorder UI minimal: record, save, play, folder, command
- make the latest take obvious
- keep automatic file naming clean and impossible to confuse
- improve “what happened?” status messages after every record/save/play
- make command actions visible: warm, loud, room, delete, export

Creative reps:

- record 3-5 short guitar or vocal ideas per week
- save only the best sections
- mark each take as raw, promising, archive, or use
- test one vocal preset per usable take

Data to collect:

- instrument used
- recording length
- preset
- whether it played back cleanly
- what felt natural
- what should be repeated

End-of-month result:

- one trusted recording workflow
- 12-20 usable local takes
- first evidence of which voice/guitar lane feels best

## Month 2: Build The Sound Taste Map

Goal: find what sounds best on Marcelo before chasing output.

Build:

- improve Sound Taste Map with active lane history
- connect Song Study, Cover Radar, and Taste Map more tightly
- add a clear “Use this lane tonight” flow
- add “avoid sameness” warnings based on recent sessions
- improve save/review flow for covers vs original seeds

Creative reps:

- test 2 cover ideas per week, only 20-30 seconds each
- test 1 original hook seed per week
- rotate lanes: dreamy guitar pop, glossy R&B, Spanish smooth, goofy charm, field-sound world
- do not make full covers unless the short version earns it

Data to collect:

- voice fit score
- memorability score
- beat feel
- tempo range
- lyric theme
- audience response if posted

End-of-month result:

- clear top 2-3 sound lanes
- fewer random covers
- stronger sense of what is “Marcelo/Luca”

## Month 3: Visual World And Field Memory

Goal: make the music feel like a world without forcing content.

Build:

- connect field recordings to song/session memory
- improve visualizer direction from latest take
- add simple visual mood presets tied to sound lanes
- add a “field sound to intro” workflow
- make visual/export notes easy to save with each take

Creative reps:

- capture 1 field sound per week: rain, road, room, trail, parking lot, night air
- pair each field sound with one guitar/vocal idea
- make 1 visual sketch per week from a take
- save photos/video references without over-editing

Data to collect:

- place/mood
- matching sound lane
- best visual color
- whether it supports cover, original, or intro

End-of-month result:

- small library of field sounds and visual directions
- first consistent visual identity
- at least 4 “world seeds” for future songs/videos

## Month 4: Fast Video Assembly

Goal: make audio + video + captions easier without becoming a full DAW/video editor.

Build:

- improve phone video + GateKPT audio pairing workflow
- make caption drafts faster and cleaner
- add export checklist: audio, visual, caption, platform, archive
- make visualizer reveal export more practical
- improve “Tonight Output” so it chooses clip type from history

Creative reps:

- record 1 clean cover/performance clip per week
- record 1 process or visual clip per week
- use Memorable Filter before posting/exporting
- keep unready clips in archive without guilt

Data to collect:

- which opening frames work
- caption style
- retention idea
- whether people comment on voice, look, song choice, or vibe

End-of-month result:

- repeatable video workflow
- less friction from recording to export
- stronger public-facing clip quality

## Month 5: First Body Of Work

Goal: move from isolated clips to a small creative world.

Build:

- improve EP map from saved sessions
- add track/idea grouping: cover, original seed, field intro, visual
- add “body of work” dashboard: strongest 5 ideas, missing pieces, next recording
- make archive review easier: best take, best lane, best audience signal

Creative reps:

- choose 5 strongest ideas from the archive
- develop 2 into fuller demos
- keep 3 as short clip/seed concepts
- test one Spanish/Spanglish line naturally
- make one visual identity pass for the body of work

Data to collect:

- strongest hook
- strongest vocal preset
- strongest visual lane
- strongest audience response
- what still feels fake or forced

End-of-month result:

- first small body of work exists
- clear shortlist of songs/seeds
- GateKPT can explain what is strongest and why

## Month 6: Polish, Publish, Decide

Goal: decide what GateKPT and the artist project become after the apartment season.

Build:

- clean the UI around the workflows that actually got used
- remove or hide features that did not help
- harden local data backups
- improve export/archive reliability
- create a summary report from six months of sessions

Creative reps:

- publish only the strongest clips
- finish at least 1 polished cover or original demo
- review all historical data
- choose the next six-month direction: music side project, serious artist push, or tool-first build

Data to review:

- best sound lane
- worst repetition trap
- best post/clip
- strongest audience comment
- best cover fit
- best original seed
- most natural visual identity

End-of-roadmap result:

- a working private creative OS
- a clear artistic direction
- a body of saved work
- a better decision system than “just post more”
- enough evidence to decide the next phase

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

Minimum viable week:

```text
2 capture sessions
1 song study
1 taste lane review
1 memorable filter decision
1 archive review
0-2 public posts
```

Ideal week:

```text
3 capture sessions
2 short cover tests
1 original hook seed
1 field sound or visual sketch
1 polished export
1 public post only if memorable
```

## Daily Apartment Rule

When tired:

```text
record one small thing, save it, write what felt good, leave
```

When energized:

```text
record, shape, compare, run memorable filter, export only if it earns it
```

When stuck:

```text
rotate lane: cover -> process -> world -> original seed -> visual reveal
```

## What Not To Do

- do not turn GateKPT into a giant DAW
- do not turn GateKPT into a social media scheduler
- do not chase every song suggestion
- do not post five clips with the same sound and same feeling
- do not over-polish charm out of the performance
- do not treat archive/practice as failure

## The Apartment Thesis

This apartment is the training room.

The work is to become fast, honest, organized, and memorable. GateKPT should make it easier to create without losing the original feeling, then use history to show what deserves more time.
