# GateKPT Marketing Playbook

## Job

GateKPT marketing has one job:

```text
make people want to enter the world without overexplaining the tool.
```

Do not sell like a startup.
Do not beg for attention.
Do not pretend the OS is finished.
Show the feeling, the sound, the process, and the person.

## Positioning

Public line:

```text
GateKPT is a creative audio project for turning raw sound into visual moments.
```

Artist line:

```text
I am building songs from guitar, voice, loops, night sounds, and pressure.
```

Website line:

```text
What does sound look like?
```

Private OS line:

```text
create fast -> save history -> learn taste -> choose better -> export when ready
```

## What To Say Less

Avoid:

- AI music app
- all-in-one platform
- revolutionary OS
- creator economy ecosystem
- changes everything
- built for everyone
- replaces DAWs
- XIV family language on public pages
- too many explanations of features

## What To Say More

Use:

- raw take
- cover pass
- guitar loop
- voice note
- field sound
- visual mood
- after-work session
- warm motel signal
- night room
- this version felt better
- I am learning what fits my voice

## Core Public Story

The human story:

```text
I work all day, come home, record one idea, shape it, and see what it becomes.
```

The artistic story:

```text
Florida night pop: guitar, R&B vocals, Spanish color, visual atmosphere, and real pressure turning into sound.
```

The product story:

```text
GateKPT is the private system that helps me keep the best ideas from disappearing.
```

## Audience

Primary:

- people who like covers and short vocal clips
- people who like seeing artists build in public
- people who like guitar, R&B, pop, Spanglish, and emotional hooks
- people who care about vibe before technical detail

Secondary:

- musicians who struggle with DAW overwhelm
- creators who like visualizers and process
- software people curious about custom creative tools

## Public Funnel

### First Touch

They see:

- short cover clip
- visualizer loop
- raw vs polished vocal
- comment reply video
- night-room process clip

They should think:

```text
This feels real. I want to see the next one.
```

### Second Touch

They visit:

- YouTube Shorts
- Instagram
- Snapchat
- gatekpt.ai

They should understand:

```text
He is building a music world, not just posting random covers.
```

### Third Touch

They stay because:

- the voice improves
- the visuals are memorable
- the song choices make sense
- the process feels human
- the comments get answered

## Repeatable Series

### 1. Raw To Chrome

Hook:

```text
raw take first, polished version second
```

Why it works:

- simple transformation
- shows GateKPT without selling it
- lets people hear the difference

### 2. After Work Cover

Hook:

```text
one familiar song, one night-room version
```

Why it works:

- human
- repeatable
- fits real life

### 3. What Does Sound Look Like?

Hook:

```text
same guitar take, different visual air
```

Feelings:

- fire
- storm
- chrome
- ocean
- night

Why it works:

- public-safe website/demo idea
- connects sound to visual identity

### 4. Comment Picks The Song

Hook:

```text
someone comments a song, Marcelo tests 15 seconds
```

Why it works:

- creates participation
- avoids forcing content ideas alone
- turns comments into direction

### 5. Song Study Night

Hook:

```text
why this song works in 20 seconds
```

Why it works:

- proves taste
- helps choose covers
- keeps the musician side intelligent without being stiff

## Cover Selection Rules

Pick songs with:

- a hook people know
- a melody that works with guitar or loop
- emotional directness
- room for Spanish color or R&B polish
- a 15-30 second section that can stand alone

Avoid songs that:

- need huge production to work
- force a vocal range that fights the voice
- are only trendy but not emotionally useful
- make every clip sound the same

## Weekly Marketing System

Monday:

- choose 3 possible covers
- save why each might fit

Tuesday:

- test the best 15 seconds
- post rough version to Snapchat or private archive

Wednesday:

- record clean version in GateKPT
- test Raw Clean vs Late Night Chrome

Thursday:

- make visualizer version
- choose best caption

Friday:

- post strongest public clip
- reply to comments

Saturday:

- livestream or longer build session
- capture field sound or visual material

Sunday:

- review comments/views/saves
- update cover signal scores
- pick next week’s lane

## GateKPT Website Job

The website should not explain everything.

It should:

- ask one question
- let people hear/see one example
- link socials
- show the mood
- make the project feel alive

Homepage thesis:

```text
What does sound look like?
```

Secondary line:

```text
Pick an air. Hear the guitar. Watch the room change.
```

Do not add:

- feature walls
- heavy product claims
- private OS screenshots unless they are beautiful and minimal
- fake pricing before the workflow is reliable

## GateKPT Music OS Marketing Job

The private OS should help Marcelo:

- pick tonight’s mission
- record one usable idea
- choose a sound lane
- generate a post pack
- log what got response
- avoid repeating the same sound too much

The app should not:

- pressure posting
- show too many unused features
- turn music into admin work

## First 30 Days Marketing Targets

Do not chase monetization.

Target:

- 12-20 short clips
- 4 visualizer demos
- 4 song-study notes
- 2 livestream tests
- 1 simple website update per week
- 1 list of songs people suggested
- 1 list of covers that actually fit Marcelo’s voice

Win condition:

```text
people start associating Marcelo with a specific sound-world.
```

## What To Measure

Track:

- views
- likes
- comments
- saves if available
- best comment
- song suggestions
- whether people mention voice, vibe, look, tool, or song choice
- whether the clip felt like Marcelo

Do not overread one post.

Look for repeated signals over 4-6 weeks.

## Brand Guardrails

Always:

- human first
- sound first
- feeling first
- tool second

Never:

- desperate
- overexplained
- fake luxury
- AI hype
- “look how genius this is”
- too many terms at once
