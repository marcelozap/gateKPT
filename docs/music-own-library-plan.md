# Own Music Library Plan

Goal: replace passive streaming with a private library of Marcelo-made music.

This is not anti-music. This is ownership.

## Rule

Make enough original audio that the default move is:

`Open my own folder. Play my own work. Keep building.`

## Why

- Spotify is gone.
- The money stays with me.
- Listening becomes training.
- My demos become memory.
- My sounds become the soundtrack for work, gym, driving, cleaning, and resting.

## Library Buckets

1. Loops
   - guitar loops
   - piano loops
   - RC-505 ideas
   - drum pockets

2. Songs
   - rough hooks
   - voice memos
   - chorus ideas
   - finished demos

3. Backgrounds
   - rain drone
   - night drive
   - balcony morning
   - warm room
   - focus pad

4. Performance Clips
   - screen-recorded GateKPT Music OS visuals
   - one-take guitar
   - vocal pass
   - loop build

5. Personal Mixes
   - gym mix
   - work focus mix
   - smoke/window mix
   - night drive mix
   - sleep/reset mix

## Weekly Target

Minimum:

- 3 short loops
- 1 rough song idea
- 1 background audio bed
- 1 personal mix export

Stretch:

- 10 audio files per week
- 1 screen-recorded visual session
- 1 downloadable pack

## File Naming

Use simple names:

`2026-06-11-night-guitar-loop-01.wav`

`2026-06-11-balcony-drone-01.wav`

`2026-06-11-work-focus-mix-01.wav`

## GateKPT Music OS Role

GateKPT Music OS should help:

- record takes
- mix sessions
- create content packs
- save taste notes
- open folders fast
- keep a growing personal archive

The app should point toward one habit:

`Record something. Save it. Listen to it later.`

## 30-Day Goal

By the end of 30 days:

- 100+ personal audio files
- 10+ loop ideas
- 5+ song seeds
- 5+ background beds
- 3+ personal mixes
- 1 folder that feels better than scrolling streaming apps

## Core Phrase

I do not need to rent music all day. I can build the room I listen to.
