# GateKPT Livestream + Content Research Plan

## Core Direction

GateKPT should help Marcelo create better music first, then export content when the idea is strong.

The public idea is simple:

```text
Watch a song become a world.
```

The private OS loop stays:

```text
create fast -> save history -> learn taste -> choose better -> export when ready
```

## What The Research Says

Short-form video is still the main discovery layer for new music, but it is not enough by itself. The strongest artist systems connect:

- short clips for discovery
- longer video or livestream for depth
- direct community touchpoints for retention
- a visible repeatable world so people remember the artist

GateKPT should not chase every trend. It should study what repeats across successful posts:

- strong first 1-3 seconds
- one clear hook or emotional turn
- a face, place, or visual identity people can remember
- a simple reason to comment
- process clips that feel human, not salesy
- familiar songs reinterpreted through a personal sound

## GateKPT Content Thesis

The artist world is not:

```text
I made a tool. Look at my features.
```

The artist world is:

```text
I am building songs from real nights, guitar, voice, loops, language, and pressure.
```

GateKPT becomes interesting when the audience can see the transformation:

```text
raw sound -> shaped take -> visual world -> post/stream moment
```

## Livestream Concept

Working title:

```text
Night Room Sessions
```

Format:

- one room
- one guitar or loop
- one visualizer mood
- one cover/hook/field sound
- one finished clip or archived idea

The stream should feel like a calm open studio, not a high-pressure concert.

Audience promise:

```text
Come sit in while the song takes shape.
```

## Stream Formats

### 1. Build A Cover

Use when there is a known song people already like.

Flow:

```text
pick song -> play raw guitar/vocal -> choose vocal lane -> record 2-3 takes -> pick best 15 seconds -> save post pack
```

GateKPT should track:

- song title
- artist
- reason it fits Marcelo
- vocal lane
- strongest hook timestamp
- whether it deserves a full cover

### 2. Raw To Chrome

Use when the hook needs polish.

Flow:

```text
raw vocal -> Raw Clean -> Late Night Chrome -> compare -> post the transformation
```

GateKPT should track:

- raw score
- processed score
- what changed
- whether polish helped or hurt

### 3. Field Sound To Song

Use when the night/world is the starting point.

Flow:

```text
rain/road/room tone -> guitar or melody -> one lyric line -> visual mood -> archive seed
```

GateKPT should track:

- place
- sound source
- emotional color
- matching guitar/vocal lane
- future song seed

### 4. Song Study Night

Use when choosing future covers or learning taste.

Flow:

```text
study 3 songs -> identify hook mechanics -> test 1 cover -> save why it worked
```

GateKPT should track:

- tempo feel
- hook entry
- lyric theme
- repetition pattern
- Spanish/Spanglish opportunity
- voice fit score
- audience signal if posted

### 5. Live Visual Painting

Use when the visualizer is the focus.

Flow:

```text
play loop/take -> choose air: fire, storm, chrome, ocean, night -> visual evolves -> save screenshot/clip
```

GateKPT should track:

- source take
- visual mood
- color lane
- clip export path
- audience reaction

## Song Research Lanes

GateKPT should help choose covers from lanes, not random impulse.

### Dreamy Catchy

Purpose:

- friendly discovery
- easy replay
- soft emotional pull

Look for:

- simple chorus
- intimate lyric
- melody that works on guitar
- sweet but not childish

### Florida Guitar Pop

Purpose:

- Dominic Fike looseness
- local identity
- guitar-first clips

Look for:

- groove in the strum
- casual vocal phrasing
- sunny/night contrast
- room for personality

### Glossy Hook R&B

Purpose:

- stronger vocal polish
- Late Night Chrome moments
- dramatic before/after clips

Look for:

- chorus within 15-30 seconds
- clear melodic lift
- simple emotional phrase
- room for delay/reverb

### Spanish Color

Purpose:

- natural Spanglish identity
- warmth and rhythm
- not forced translation

Look for:

- one phrase worth carrying
- vowels that suit the voice
- rhythm that feels smooth
- emotional directness

### Goofy Charm

Purpose:

- make the artist human
- prevent the world from getting too serious
- invite comments

Look for:

- playful lyric twist
- casual face/camera moment
- not cringe, not fake
- short clip that feels like personality

## What GateKPT Should Build Next

### Near Term

Build a `Research Night` panel:

- add song to study
- choose lane
- write why it fits
- score voice fit
- score content fit
- save hook notes

Build a `Stream Prep` panel:

- tonight's mission
- source sound
- cover/hook target
- visual air
- post goal

Build a `Post After Stream` panel:

- best 15 seconds
- caption idea
- platform target
- clip exported yes/no
- audience response later

### Later

Build a `Taste Radar`:

- which lanes were used too much
- which songs got better comments
- which tempos fit best
- which visual colors matched best
- which covers should become originals

## Weekly Operating Rhythm

Monday:

- study 3 songs
- pick 1 cover test

Tuesday:

- record a raw guitar/vocal take
- save the best 15 seconds

Wednesday:

- apply vocal preset
- compare raw vs polished

Thursday:

- make a visualizer clip
- export one short

Friday:

- post the strongest clip
- respond to comments

Saturday:

- livestream or longer session
- field recording / visual capture

Sunday:

- review what worked
- update song study scores
- choose next lane

## Content Rules

Use:

- one song
- one sound
- one visual
- one post

Avoid:

- overposting everything
- explaining the whole OS every time
- acting like the tool is finished if the workflow is still growing
- fake luxury
- forcing Spanish where it does not belong
- making every take heavily tuned

## The Long Game

The livestream is not only content.

It is a public creative ritual:

```text
Marcelo builds the artist world in real time.
```

The audience is not only watching music.

They are watching taste become visible.
