# GateKPT Personal World OS Direction

GateKPT is personal first.

It is not trying to be a generic DAW or a public music app. It is Marcelo's custom creative operating system for becoming fluent across music, language, culture, travel, performance, and video.

The recorder is one room. It should stay simple.

The larger OS should answer:

- What am I learning?
- What did I hear?
- Who did I meet?
- What phrase, food, rhythm, or story should become a song?
- What did I record?
- What can I post?
- What can I practice next?

## Core Rooms

1. Performance / Reveal
   Live sound becomes visual terrain.

2. Song Builder
   Rhythm, harmony, voice, and RC-505 ideas become songs.

3. Recorder
   Fast clean takes, simple capture lanes, post-ready clip export.

4. Lyric Vault
   Hooks, fragments, titles, emotional lines.

5. Visual Room
   Audio-reactive painting, cover direction, clip identity.

6. World Memory
   People, languages, food, places, phrases, and cultural moments that can become music.

7. Export Memory
   What was posted, where it went, what version made it.

## World Memory Rule

GateKPT should help turn travel and culture into art without flattening it.

It should remember:

- phrase and pronunciation
- person/place
- food or ritual
- rhythm/instrument
- possible lyric
- video idea
- what to practice

## Product Guardrails

- Keep recording mode simple.
- Expand the OS around the user's real creative life.
- Do not add generic social media bloat.
- Do not fake features.
- Every room should produce memory, practice, sound, or a postable artifact.
- The app should feel like a passport, studio, notebook, and stage in one private system.
