# GateKPT Cover Video Direction

GateKPT is moving toward a simple creator loop:

1. Record a phone video performance.
2. Record cleaner audio through Scarlett / RC-505.
3. Shape the audio with simple commands.
4. Sync the clean audio to the phone video.
5. Export a post-ready clip.
6. Add a restrained visual/code layer when the core clip works.

## Product Center

GateKPT should help make fast music covers and performance clips feel more polished without turning into a full DAW.

The core user is an artist who already has musical ideas and wants fewer technical steps between playing, saving, shaping, and posting.

## Current Working Surface

- Capture lanes: Full Loop, Drums, Guitar / Keys, Vocals.
- Clean take export from GateKPT audio.
- Phone video pairing with latest/selected take.
- Post clip export with sync offset.
- Simple command edits: warmer, louder, room, cyber vocal, delete last.

## Next Technical Priorities

1. Clap/hit sync detection.
   Detect a loud transient in phone video audio and GateKPT audio, then suggest the sync offset.

2. Export presets.
   Keep this small: vertical short, same shape, archive.

3. Visual layer pass.
   Add optional terrain/waveform overlay export for clips. It should feel like sound becoming art, not flashy effects.

4. Take confidence.
   Show whether the take is likely post-ready: duration, RMS, clipping, silence gaps.

5. Voice effect chain.
   Expand text commands for vocals: intimate, cyber, room, wide, raw, warmer, brighter.

## Guardrails

- Do not rebuild into a full DAW.
- Do not add more controls until the post workflow is reliable.
- Do not make the UI feel like a toy music app.
- Preserve the working recorder path.
- Make every new feature answer: does this help finish/post the video?
