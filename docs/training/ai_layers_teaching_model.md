# AI Layers Teaching Model

GateKPT should teach AI from the closest human action outward.

## Core Principle

Start where the reader starts.

Do not begin with power, chips, data centers, or infrastructure unless the piece is specifically about infrastructure. For general teaching, begin with the moment someone uses AI.

## Preferred Order

1. `Input`: the person types, uploads, asks, or gives a constraint.
2. `Tokens`: the system turns words into processable chunks.
3. `Context`: files, memory, search, instructions, and retrieved material shape the request.
4. `Models`: the model generates from the input and context.
5. `Tools`: the system may search, calculate, read files, call functions, or act.
6. `Chips`: the work runs on physical hardware.
7. `Power`: electricity, cooling, buildings, and grid access make the system possible.

## Why This Order

This is inductive and intuitive. The reader understands the surface first, then learns the hidden system.

The opposite order can be technically correct but emotionally distant. If the first thing a new reader sees is grid infrastructure, they may not connect it to their own use of AI.

## Good Teaching Frame

> What happens after you hit send?

That question is strong because everyone understands the action. GateKPT can then reveal the deeper system step by step.

## Bad Teaching Frame

> AI starts with power infrastructure.

This is true at a systems level, but it is not the best first doorway for a general reader.

## Tone

Use wonder without losing precision. The reader should feel:

- "I know this surface."
- "I did not realize that much was happening."
- "Now the system makes more sense."
