# GateKPT Artist Content Engine

## Core Lane

Florida Night R&B.

Audience promise:

> Come watch me build the sound: glossy vocals, Spanish/English covers, field sounds, visuals, and pressure turning into music.

This is not random covers. Every post should point back to the world: night light, orange glow, pressure, vocals, field texture, and a real artist improving in public.

## Audience

Primary:

- 18-34 music fans who like melodic R&B, pop, reggaeton, late-night songs, and emotional covers.
- Tory Lanez / Rauw Alejandro / Lil Tecca-adjacent listeners.
- Spanish/English listeners.
- People who like seeing an artist become in public.

Secondary:

- Florida / Central Florida people.
- Gym, pressure, recovery, and self-improvement people.
- Creators who like behind-the-scenes systems.
- Visual identity / fashion people.

What they should feel:

- I found this guy early.
- He has a real world.
- He is getting better every week.
- I want to see where this goes.

## Five Pillars

1. The Cover: 20-60 seconds of singing. This is the main discovery content.
2. The Process: warmup, recording, mistakes, GateKPT screen, vocal preset test.
3. The World: night roads, orange glow, trails, rain, parking lots, gym recovery, car voice notes.
4. The Look: outfits, mirror shots, details, black/orange/chrome palette, artist in the training phase.
5. The Build: XIV/GateKPT as the personal music OS. Use lightly, not too nerdy every post.

## Visual Rules

Use:

- warm orange light
- phone light / car light
- low light with face visible
- black shirt or jacket
- palms, roads, lakes, parking lots
- wet pavement after rain
- simple room setup with mic/headphones
- vertical 9:16 video

Avoid:

- messy background
- overhead bathroom lighting
- footage too dark to see the face
- random captions that do not connect to the world

Visual formula:

```text
face + night light + lyric caption + clean vocal
```

## GateKPT Role

GateKPT gives the signature sound.

- Late Night Chrome: main polished lead.
- Silk Synth: hookier synth-lead parts.
- Luna Pop: Spanish/Rauw-style smooth vocal.
- Cloud Doubles: adlibs/background layers.
- Raw Clean: honest comparison.

Recurring series:

```text
Raw vocal vs Late Night Chrome
```

## Platform Roles

YouTube:

- Archive and deeper fan conversion.
- Shorts 2-4 per week.
- Full cover/process video every 2 weeks.

Instagram:

- Identity, visuals, social proof.
- Reels 3 per week.
- Stories daily when possible.
- One lookbook/carousel per week.

Snapchat:

- Closeness and daily process.
- Rough takes, gym recovery, night drive snippets, field sounds, quick voice notes.

TikTok:

- Fastest testing ground.
- 3-5 per week if possible.
- Hook testing, raw vs processed, field sound to song idea.

## Weekly Rhythm

- Monday: pick cover. Record rough Snapchat/TikTok.
- Tuesday: practice. Post process clip.
- Wednesday: record clean vocal in GateKPT. Test Late Night Chrome.
- Thursday: post raw vs processed clip.
- Friday: post best cover clip to IG/TikTok/YouTube Shorts.
- Saturday: capture visuals/field sounds. Post story dump.
- Sunday: review analytics. Save best clip to website/archive. Pick next song.

## After Work Sequence

1. Eat, shower, reset neck.
2. Pick one cover only.
3. Record 3 phone takes.
4. Record clean vocal in GateKPT.
5. Apply Late Night Chrome.
6. Export one clip.
7. Post to Snapchat first, then IG/TikTok/YouTube if usable.
8. Log song, preset, clip posted, what worked.
